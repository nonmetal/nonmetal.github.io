I am a Ph.D. student at the Department of Information Physics & Computing, Graduate School of Information Science and Technology, [The University of Tokyo](https://www.u-tokyo.ac.jp/en/), advised by [Yuki Saito](https://taroithink.github.io/).

My research focuses on speech and audio processing, including:
- Speech Synthesis / Voice Conversion
- Spoken Language Models / Speech Conversation
- Large-Language Models / Agentic AI
- Generative models (Autoregressive, Diffusion model)
- Self-Supervised Learning

Previously, I received my Master's degree from The University of Tokyo (advised by [Daisuke Saito](https://sites.google.com/site/daborobotics/)) and my Bachelor's degree from [Sogang University](https://www.sogang.ac.kr/en/).

I have industry research experience at [Spellbrush](https://spellbrush.com/), [CoeFont](https://coefont.cloud/), [Cygames](https://www.cygames.co.jp/en/), [Preferred Networks](https://www.preferred.jp/en/), [NTT R&D](https://www.rd.ntt/e/), and [DeNA](https://dena.com/intl/).
